Data Methodology, Denominators & Boundaries

        
        

          

            
1. Record Identity & Deduplication Rule

            
Per DATA_CONTRACT.md, record identity is strictly the 2-tuple (source_type, source_id). Names, record_ref and array positions are non-unique conveniences. When an incoming record has a higher revision (Rev 2 > Rev 1), its source payload is updated in full without inflating activity counts. When a lower or equal revision arrives, it is ignored.

          


          

            
2. Explicit Metrics & Denominators

            
    
              
  • Selected Records (44): The complete sample of 30 calls and 14 meetings. Activity is restricted to 21 August 2026 Asia/Kolkata.
    • 
              
  • Usable Transcript Coverage (17/44 -> 18/44, 40.9%): Defined strictly as records with non-empty text (calls with transcript string, meetings with non-empty turns array). Denominator is total 44 records.
    • 
              
  • Call Connection Rate (33.3%): Calculated as calls with is_connected = true (10) divided by calls with non-null is_connected status (30). Labelled as source-reported classification.
    • 
            

          


          

            
3. Distinction: Call Reps vs. Meeting Organisers

            
Call reps (Arun, Leela, Meera, Ravi, Dev, Kiran) actively dialled prospects. In contrast, meeting organisers (team08-team14) are calendar owners who may not be attendees or salespersons. The application separates them in filters and reporting.

          


          

            
4. Deliberate Boundaries & Honest Uncertainty

            
No authoritative CRM deal values, account history, or employee directories are provided. The application does not invent pipeline numbers or unverified corporate affiliations. Unrecorded background calls are kept in an intake state without fabricated conclusions.
